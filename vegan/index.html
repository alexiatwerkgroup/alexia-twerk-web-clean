The notify control, explained

Vegan's page has no released videos yet, so the "Notify me if launched" control on this page does exactly one thing: it emails you when the catalog goes live. It doesn't unlock anything, reserve a discount, or give early access — there's nothing to access until the first clip is published.

Why there's no release date yet

Twerkhub doesn't publish a launch date before a catalog is finished, because every clip needs its duration, resolution and preview data in place before the page switches from "coming soon" to a live grid — the same rule applied to every model already on the site.

"An announced date that slips does more damage than no date at all — so this page says 'coming soon' until there's an actual catalog to show."— Twerkhub team

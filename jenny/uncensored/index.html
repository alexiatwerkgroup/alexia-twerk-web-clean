/ faq

Quick answers.

What do I get?

Access to the original full-quality video — watch online and download it where included.

Coins vs tokens?

Tokens are earned on Twerkhub and unlock all 63 full non-explicit videos and the 30 short clips (500 tokens each). Coins are bought separately and unlock only Jenny’s 24 explicit videos: 1 coin per started minute. Buy them one by one or get the complete Hot Pack for US$18.25 in USDT, 50% off.

How fast is access?

As soon as payment is confirmed (usually within minutes after sending the receipt on Discord). Sign in at twerkhub.lat with the same email you gave us.

How do I unlock the full archive?

Each of the 63 full non-explicit videos and 30 short clips costs 500 earned tokens. Only the separate 24-video explicit collection is paid with coins or the discounted USDT Hot Pack.

Is this for adults only?

Yes. 18+ only. You must be of legal age to view this content.

Inside the Jenny collection.

93 originals · one of Twerkhub's founding catalogs

One of the site's original catalogs

Jenny's 93 originals make up one of the first catalogs built on Twerkhub, which is why her page still appears as the default "VIP" link in the site's navigation on several sections. The access model is the same as every catalog added since: preview with sound, duration and resolution shown upfront, unlock by the minute.

What stayed consistent as the site grew

As Twerkhub added more models — Lolita, Tainara, Abbi and the rest — the per-minute coin pricing and download-on-unlock structure from Jenny's catalog carried over unchanged. It's the baseline the newer catalogs are built against.

"We didn't redesign the access model every time we added a model — if it worked on the first catalog, it stayed the standard for the next one."— Twerkhub team

/ faq

Quick answers.

What do I get?

Access to the original, full-quality video — you can watch it online and download it.

Coins vs tokens?

Coins cost US$0.50 each. Each Tainara video is charged at 1 coin per started minute; the total appears on its card. Tokens unlock eligible short clips.

How fast is access?

As soon as your payment is confirmed (usually within minutes, after you send the receipt on Discord). Sign in at twerkhub.lat with the same email you gave us.

Why are the short clips not for sale?

They're under 15 seconds, so they're a bonus you earn with tokens instead of paying.

Is this for adults only?

Yes. 18+ only. You must be of legal age to view this content.

/ about this catalog

Inside the Tainara collection.

96 originals · the largest single catalog on Twerkhub

Volume with individual access, not a bundle

Tainara's catalog holds 96 originals, 93 of them sold individually at the same duration-based coin pricing used across Twerkhub. It's the largest single catalog on the site, which means more variety in length, resolution and setting than a smaller set can offer.

Why a bigger catalog changes how people browse

With this many clips, most visitors don't watch in order — they use the preview grid to find a specific length or style first, then unlock from there. The universal preview and metadata system (duration, resolution) exists specifically so a catalog this size stays easy to scan.

"Past a certain size, a catalog needs real filtering, not just a bigger grid — that's why every clip here carries the same duration and resolution tags, however many there are."— Twerkhub team

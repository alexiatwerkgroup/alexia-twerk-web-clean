/ faq

Quick answers.

What do I get?

Access to the original, full-quality video — you can watch it online and download it.

Coins vs tokens?

Coins are bought (1 coin = 1 video, minimum purchase 20 coins, $0.50 each). Tokens are points you earn on Twerkhub and only unlock the short clips.

How fast is access?

As soon as your payment is confirmed (usually within minutes, after you send the receipt on Discord). Sign in at twerkhub.lat with the same email you gave us.

Why are the short clips not for sale?

They're under 15 seconds, so they're a bonus you earn with tokens instead of paying.

Is this for adults only?

Yes. 18+ only. You must be of legal age to view this content.

Why it's one pack, not per-video pricing

As Twerkhub's founder, Alexia runs her own catalog differently from the rest of the roster: instead of coin pricing by the minute, her 63 videos are sold as a single $19.99 pack covering everything at once. It's the one catalog on the site set up this way.

What that says about the rest of Twerkhub

Every other model's catalog — Jenny's, Tainara's, Lolita's — uses per-video coin pricing because that's what the majority of visitors preferred once both options existed. The pack format stayed on Alexia's page as the original approach the site launched with.

"The pack option stayed live on the founder's own page on purpose — it's where Twerkhub started, and per-video pricing only became the default once we saw how people actually wanted to buy."— Twerkhub team

Curious how the per-video model works instead? Jenny's 93-video catalog and Tainara's 96 originals both use duration-based coin pricing.

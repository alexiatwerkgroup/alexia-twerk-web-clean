Brazilian funk and the dancers who shared it online defined an unforgettable chapter of early YouTube. The local collection indexes 1,061 folders and 12,565 video files, preserving a record of material that is increasingly hard to find.

The collection is being organized as a searchable historical archive. Folder and file totals below are local inventory counts; public cards use randomized generic labels and broad keyword categories.

Original names, filenames and media are kept out of the searchable directory. A separate sample channel demonstrates a video page using preview clips.
